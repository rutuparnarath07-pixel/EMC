EMC 
AT A GLANCE
Foundation and Impact
Ecometrix Consultants Pvt. Ltd. (EMC) is a leading engineering and technical consultancy headquartered in 
Bhubaneswar, Odisha. Established in the year 2015, EMC delivers end-to-end infrastructure development,
engineering & project management solutions spanning water supply, used water management, stormwater 
drainage, urban infrastructure planning, GIS & IT, transportation,  digital solutions and beyond.
