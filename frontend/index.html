Ecometrix Consultants Pvt. Ltd. (EMC) is a leading engineering and technical consultancy headquartered in Bhubaneswar, Odisha. Established in the year 2015, EMC delivers end-to-end infrastructure development, engineering & project management solutions spanning water supply, used water management, stormwater drainage, urban infrastructure planning, GIS & IT, transportation, digital solutions and beyond.
