1. Executive Summary & Scope

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") governs the collection, storage, processing, transfer, and protection of personal data and digital technical assets by Ecometrix Consultants Pvt. Ltd. (EMC) ("Company", "We", "Us", "Our"), a leading multidisciplinary engineering and technical consultancy registered under the Companies Act, India, with headquarters in Odisha.

This Policy applies to all individuals, corporate entities, municipal authorities, project partners, job applicants, and visitors accessing our website (www.ecometrix.co.in), digital portals, GIS dashboards, and related consultancy platforms ("Services").

Key Commitment: EMC strictly enforces zero-sale policies on personal data. All data collected is solely processed for legitimate corporate consultancy purposes, institutional project execution, regulatory compliance, and client communication.

2. Statutory Framework Compliance

EMC complies with all applicable national and international data privacy laws and standards, including but not limited to:

  • Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act), 2023 (India).
  • Information Technology Act, 2000 and IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.
  • ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management Guidelines for corporate infrastructure data.
  • Data protection requirements mandated by international donor agencies (World Bank, JICA, Asian Development Bank) for multilateral consultancy projects.

3. Categories of Data Collected

In the course of providing consultancy services and operating our website, EMC collects the following categories of information:

  • Personal Identification Data: Full name, professional designation, organizational affiliation, official email address, telephone numbers submitted via contact or proposal forms.
  • Employment & Career Data: Curriculum vitae (CV), educational credentials, work experience history, professional certifications, and references uploaded by job applicants.
  • Technical & Geospatial Data: System IP addresses, browser specifications, operating system details, geolocation metadata (aggregated), and spatial telemetry for GIS web dashboard sessions.
  • Project & Tender Inquiries: Tendering details, RFP documentation, scope specifications, and corporate communication logs.

4. Purpose of Data Processing

EMC processes collected data strictly under lawful bases, including contract execution, legal obligations, and legitimate corporate interests:

  • Executing technical consultancy contracts, DPR preparation, and PMC project supervision.
  • Processing job applications and conducting background verification for engineering personnel.
  • Responding to corporate inquiries, municipal RFPs, and international technical tenders.
  • Maintaining cybersecurity controls, preventing unauthorized access, and auditing web portal activity.
  • Fulfilling statutory reporting to government departments, municipal corporations, and regulatory agencies.

5. Data Security & Encryption Controls

EMC deploys enterprise-grade administrative, physical, and technical security safeguards to prevent data loss, unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration:

  • Encryption Standards: Data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption; server repositories enforce AES-256 bit encryption at rest.
  • Access Controls: Role-based access control (RBAC) restricting project repositories to authorized key personnel.
  • Network Protection: Firewalls, intrusion prevention systems (IPS), and automated threat intelligence scanners protecting cloud and on-premise infrastructure.

6. Retention & Disposal Policy

Personal and project data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected or to satisfy statutory requirement limits (e.g., 8 years under Indian Corporate Law for contractual records). Upon expiration of retention periods, data is securely sanitized or anonymized using NIST SP 800-88 compliant media erasure guidelines.

7. Disclosure to Third Parties

EMC does not sell, trade, or rent personal data. We disclose information only to:

  • Government bodies, state water boards, and municipal corporations when required by law or contract.
  • Multilateral funding agencies (JICA, World Bank, ADB) as mandatory audit disclosures for funded projects.
  • Trusted IT infrastructure vendors subject to strict Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) ensuring equivalent security controls.

8. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses essential technical cookies and anonymous analytical cookies to optimize performance, remember session parameters, and capture aggregated visitor statistics (such as the Live Visitor Counter). Users can control cookie preferences via browser settings.

9. Data Principal Rights

Under the DPDP Act 2023, Data Principals possess the following rights regarding their personal data held by EMC:

  • Right to access summary of personal data being processed.
  • Right to correction, completion, and updating of inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure of personal data where statutory retention is no longer mandatory.
  • Right to nominate an individual to exercise rights in case of incapacity.
